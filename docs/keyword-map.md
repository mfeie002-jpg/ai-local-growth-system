## Cannibalisation rules

1. `/ai-business-audit` owns the cross-functional lead magnet and digital-growth diagnostic.
2. `/website-audit` owns website check, analysis and website-audit intent.
3. `/seo-analyse` owns SEO audit, analysis and check intent.
4. `/ai-visibility` owns AI visibility, GEO, LLMO and ChatGPT-SEO explanations.
5. `/automation` owns CRM and marketing-workflow assessment, not generic “AI automation”.
6. `/leistungen` explains the offer ladder; it must not duplicate each diagnostic landing page.
7. `/fuer-kmu` qualifies audience fit; it is not a second service catalogue.
8. Informational “how to improve” queries belong under `/insights`.
9. Do not launch `/business-health-check` as a thin synonym for `/ai-business-audit`. Add it only after a distinct scope and evidence exist.
10. Add industry or location pages only with a verified use case and substantial unique content; no doorway pages.

## Positioning implication

Public evidence shows that “website audit”, “SEO analysis” and AI-visibility categories are already populated by scanners, agencies and software products. itsfeierabend.ch should not claim to be:

- a generic full-service AI marketing agency;
- another free SEO scanner;
- the first Swiss AI-visibility product;
- a real-time monitoring SaaS unless the production product supports it;
- a provider of guaranteed Google or AI-answer rankings.

The defensible message from the evidence is a transparent digital business diagnostic that separates:

- automatically observed signals;
- information supplied by the company;
- preliminary estimates;
- external enrichment;
- manual expert review.

The output should prioritise visibility, conversion, tracking, CRM and automation work by business impact and dependency.

## Target-audience use

### Primary

Swiss owner-led service SMEs and specialised B2B firms that already have a website, meaningful lead value and no reliable view of where demand leaks between visibility, enquiry, CRM and follow-up.

### Secondary

- agencies, consultants and IT providers needing a diagnostic/partner entry point;
- multi-location service businesses;
- SMEs already spending on SEO or advertising without reliable measurement and follow-up.

### Later or unsuitable at launch

- ecommerce until the methodology covers catalogue and checkout analysis;
- large enterprises until procurement and security controls mature;
- very small operators that cannot support a high-touch engagement;
- firms asking for guaranteed search or AI rankings;
- generic AI-transformation projects without a defined process.

## Paid-search gate

Do not publish campaigns from this map.

Paid testing requires:

- validated Swiss volume and CPC from Semrush or Google Ads Keyword Planner;
- a dedicated, working landing page;
- reliable audit-completion and qualified-lead events;
- CRM attribution and lead-quality status;
- protection against conversions fired by loading a result or thank-you URL directly;
- explicit approval for campaign, budget, bid and ad publication.
