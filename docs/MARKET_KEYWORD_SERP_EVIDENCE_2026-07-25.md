## 2. Market conclusion

There is a credible Swiss market for a diagnostic-first offer, but the easy-sounding categories are already crowded:

- “Website check/audit” is occupied by free scanners and agency lead magnets.
- “SEO analysis/audit” is highly commercial and saturated with free or low-friction audits.
- “AI visibility/GEO/LLMO” has quickly become a crowded agency and software category.
- “Digitalisierungscheck” is established but often means document workflows, ERP/DMS or general process digitisation.
- “Business audit” is semantically dangerous because Swiss results heavily overlap statutory audit, assurance and accounting.

The defensible position is therefore not “another SEO agency” and not “the first AI visibility tool.” It is:

> **A transparent digital business diagnostic for Swiss SMEs that separates measured signals, owner inputs and expert judgement, then prioritises visibility, conversion, tracking, CRM and automation work by business impact.**

That cross-functional diagnostic is the gap. Most visible competitors specialise in one silo or use a free audit only as a front door into generic agency services.

## 3. Swiss demand context

- The Swiss Federal Statistical Office states that more than 99% of Swiss businesses are SMEs. Source: [BFS — Kleine und mittlere Unternehmen](https://www.bfs.admin.ch/bfs/de/home/statistiken/industrie-dienstleistungen/unternehmen-beschaeftigte/wirtschaftsstruktur-unternehmen/kmu.html).
- The Swiss federal SME portal reports that 55% of Swiss SMEs had integrated AI into work processes in 2024, citing an AXA/Sotomo labour-market study. The same page stresses that human framing and quality data remain necessary. Source: [KMU-Portal — Die Chancen der künstlichen Intelligenz](https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/die-chancen-der-kuenstlichen-intelligenz).
- The federal SME portal says Swiss SMEs still struggle to embed AI effectively in strategy and recommends starting with defined use cases, a data/process inventory and impact measurement. Source: [KMU-Portal — Fünf Tipps, wie man KI wirksam im Unternehmen einsetzt](https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/fuenf-tipps-wie-man-ki-wirksam-im-unternehmen-einsetzt).

Implication: “AI” is not enough as a differentiator. The offer should sell **clarity, prioritisation and measured implementation readiness**, using AI where it improves the diagnostic.

## 8. Target-audience priority

### Primary

**Swiss owner-led service SMEs and B2B service firms with an existing website, meaningful lead value and no reliable view of where digital demand leaks.**

Best first-fit traits:

- roughly 5–50 employees or an equivalent operationally mature owner-led team;
- existing website, Google profile and some inbound/outbound activity;
- leads matter more than ecommerce checkout volume;
- multiple hand-offs between website, email/phone, CRM and follow-up;
- management can fund implementation when the audit demonstrates a priority.

High-fit vertical families to validate first:

- property, renovation and local professional services;
- established trades and field-service firms;
- medical/local practices where claims, privacy and reputation can be handled carefully;
- consultancies and specialised B2B providers.

### Secondary

- Agencies, consultants and IT providers needing a partner/white-label diagnostic.
- Multi-location local businesses that need local visibility, reputation and lead-routing analysis.
- SMEs already spending on SEO/Ads but lacking clean tracking and conversion follow-up.

### Later

- Gastronomy and very small local operators, once a lower-touch audit is proven economically.
- Larger enterprises, once procurement, security, data-processing and stakeholder reporting are mature.
- E-commerce, once the methodology covers catalogue, merchandising, checkout and revenue analytics in depth.

### Avoid at launch

- pre-revenue ideas without a working website or implementation budget;
- firms seeking guaranteed Google or ChatGPT rankings;
- generic “AI transformation” work without a defined business process;
- low-value consumer leads where a high-touch expert audit cannot pay back;
- regulated or enterprise work whose security/legal requirements exceed the current delivery system.

- Business impact and implementation dependencies, not vanity scores.
- Swiss market, privacy and local-trust checks.
- No claim that AI systems will recommend or rank a client.
- Human review where judgement is required.
